A stack is the repository it is named for and every rdf-array repository its runtime dependencies reach, as the forks' build files pin them (checked by tools/manifest-check.py); a repository shared by several stacks appears in each. Issues are filed by a title prefix, <repo>: …, and labelled repo, cross-repo or spec; a cross-repo issue may open with a Repos: a, b line.
